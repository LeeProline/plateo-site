Exercise guides

See it before you lift it.

How to set up, what to do and what to avoid.

Back squat

Target: thighs and glutes

Set-up

  • Set the bar on your upper back, not your neck, and hold it with hands just outside your shoulders.
  • Feet about shoulder-width apart, toes turned out a little.
  • Take a breath, brace your tummy, and look slightly ahead.

Do

  • Do: Sit down and back, as if onto a low chair.
  • Do: Keep your knees tracking in line with your toes.
  • Do: Keep your chest up and your whole foot on the floor.
  • Do: Stand up by pushing the floor away, and keep your tempo smooth: about 3 seconds down, a short pause, 1 second up.

Avoid

  • Avoid: Knees caving in as you stand up.
  • Avoid: Heels lifting off the floor.
  • Avoid: Rounding your lower back at the bottom.

Tip: use a weight you can control for every rep, and stop a rep or two before you lose your form.
